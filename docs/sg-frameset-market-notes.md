# Singapore used frameset market notes (BICII)

SAMPLE research summary for Frame Atlas pricing assist. Cleared-price bands are
plausible estimates anchored to listing asks seen in a Nov 2026 research pass —
not a live feed and not a buy offer.

## Channels

1. **Authorized retail** — Tay Junction, Treknology3 / Speedon5, Cannasia, Bikes n Bites, Rodalink, Specialized stores. Previous-year clearance caps used asks.
2. **Peer-to-peer** — Carousell (public record), Togoparts, Facebook brand groups. Asks sticky; cleared often ~10–25% under ask. “1st owner, local receipt” is a premium.
3. **Grey / regional** — Echo-style photo-approve-then-ship imports; China/Taiwan open-mold carbon; Polygon via Rodalink; Decathlon sets the floor for tired completes.

Folding (especially Brompton) is a separate liquid market because of HDB / transit use.

## What moves price in SG

Lead with: size, brake, axle, BB family, tyre clearance, proprietary post/cockpit/axles present. Then provenance and carbon condition. Humidity shows as seized posts, rusty bolts, and creaking press-fit BBs more than “carbon rot.”

Liquidity traps: rim-brake carbon, BB90/BBright/odd T47 shells, L/XL, pre-UDH FS with dead shock, missing aero post, Super Boost 157.

E-bike note: mounts ≠ legal PAB. EN 15194 + ≤250 W + 25 km/h cut-out + ≤20 kg unladen — a carbon road + mid-drive kit usually fails registration.

## Competitors (consignment / used)

Simple Bicycles / Cadence Carbon, Carbon Craft X (inspection + inspected used), Echo Cycling, Autumn Angel Sports, DCBikes (FS cash cap S$1,000), CycleYard, MP Goh, Mr Bicycles (new chromoly anchor), Brompton specialists (808, BZ).
